# Test what remains after the direct dimer reduction

Completed at bounded reduction and implementation scope. C88 owns the
surviving essential grid, polynomial coefficient and positive-prefix
Pfaffian proofs. ME owns the independent edge audit, charged sparse
comparison and retained import/source-review failures. Known methods
explain the measured improvement over the dense evaluator; no advantage
over optimized general sparse methods is established. The possible
constant-order formula remains unresolved. The mask-only signed route
stays planar matchgate/FKT, so TODO50 owns the actual controlled-arithmetic
redirection. All four board tasks are reviewed and closed. The original
brief follows; its conditional follow-ups are superseded by that decision.

C87/DM close the explicit-construction gap: a nonzero all-bit coefficient
on the rotated-zigzag family equals a positive weighted perfect-matching
count, and its graph contains a growing grid minor. They do not exclude
forced matching structure, a compact scalar recurrence or an exact closed
formula. Do not claim an advantage from the smaller graph alone, and do
not repeat a broad known-FKT size pilot.

First audit the actual weighted matching problem after removing forced
edges, vertices and edges that belong to no perfect matching. The raw
grid-minor certificate need not survive these reductions. For the all-bit
family define D_pi(z)=sum_M z^c(M), where c counts interface edges. Derive
whether D_pi(1/2) factors or has a compact recurrence for the explicit
permutations. Along either residual carry path, a chosen set of interface
endpoints permits internal matching only when every remaining gap has
even length; then that internal path matching is unique. This is an
explicit combinatorial starting point, not a proved cheap algorithm for
the two coupled orders. Charge representation construction and symbolic
coefficient sizes.

Use algebra and a few bounded exact fixtures first. Compare any proposed
formula with the direct dimer helper and independent tiny arithmetic.
Freeze the family and all query conventions; vary only its growth
parameter. Preserve a failed factorization or recurrence instead of
retrofitting it to the measured rows. General matching hardness and local
W rank do not establish hardness for this family. A finite cyclic
Thue--Morse autocorrelation is not its infinite limiting recurrence, and
two constant-bond sequences in different variable orders need not retain
constant contraction width.

If the positive-dimer reduction leaves no useful new prediction, stop
optimizing that known method. A bounded next discriminator may use an
extensive predetermined mixture of equal-mask 00 and 11 factors, after
global-complement, highest-bit, pin/equality and ignored-pivot reductions.
Derive two supported global assignments with opposite weights and an
independently checkable nonzero coefficient before calling it a signed-
cancellation example. A negative global sign is insufficient. Keep the
all-bit query as a direct-dimer control. A fixed number of altered factors
already admits a constant-size expansion into known contractions; include
that baseline rather than presenting it as a breakthrough.

Only if a concrete gap survives these audits, register a bounded matched
time/peak-allocation comparison with the strongest direct formula,
arithmetic or weighted-automaton contraction, and optimized exact tensor
elimination. Charge input/circuit setup, ordering/recognition, exact bit
lengths, caches/temporary buffers and final extraction. Compare identical
outputs. Polynomial-memory enumeration with exponential time is already
a baseline, and rational-entry counts are not bytes.

Do not carry the two-independent-operand promise into actual shared-
scratch modular arithmetic or arbitrary CNOT interfaces. Such an extension
requires its own local tensor, parity/matchgate-identity and clean/full-
space audit. It is a possible redirection if the scalar family exhausts
its value, not an already authorized conclusion from graph geometry.

Use the authorized board swarm with one canonical writer. Core first for
scientific execution, predictions before every run, bounded allocations
under TODO34, and actual source/report/exit inspection. DM's reference
defects require a carry-sensitive control and a permutation-sensitive
reference check: many symmetric masks hide missing incoming carries.
Preserve all failed sources and logs. TODO42 remains separate.
