## Object

C102's fixture: ToffoliModExp(N, 2, n_exp=1).u_a(exp[0], 2), with n =
N.bit_length(), N odd, and q = 3n + 5 wires.
- Every wire is free (the full dirty space).
- f(y) is bit x_0 of U(y).
- W(f) is the Walsh sparsity of ±f, which C8 identifies with the final PPS
  support for Z_{x0}.

C102 states its fixture as "ToffoliModExp(N, 2, n_exp=1).u_a(ctrl, 2),
observable x0, full dirty space, q = 3n + 5 qubits" (§7).
- It is one block, and final support rather than the propagation peak.
- Multi-block builds are excluded (§10 gap 4).
- The ceilings hold for any multiplier constant a coprime to N in the same
  construction (Theorem 3.3; u_a needs a⁻¹ mod N); the lower bounds are for
  a = 2.
- The same-object statements for C8, C89, C92, C30 and the TX52 lemma are
  quoted in derivation §7.

The block normal form and the slice dynamics (Lemmas B and C) rest on C89's
macro equations, which are the same object (§2, §4).

## Results

- **Stage reduction (proved, §2 Lemma 1).** U runs three stages: the
  controlled multiplier, the cswaps, and the inverse multiplier.
  - f = x0 ⊕ e·(x0 ⊕ g), where g is bit b_0 after the multiplier stage. The
    cswap(e, x_0, b_0) is what brings g into x_0.
  - The inverse-multiplier stage never affects f.
  - g1 denotes g at e = 1.
- **Ceilings (proved, §3 Theorem 3.3; any a coprime to N, N < 2^n, n ≥ 2).**
  - deg₂ f ≤ 3n + 2 = q − 3.
  - W(f) = 2·spar(±g1) + 2 ≤ 2^(3n+3) + 2, and W(f) is even.
  - The proof carries C92's symmetries P and J (P is C30's w = b_msb ⊕ anc)
    to C8's object via the stage reduction and a balance argument. That
    transfer is new here; the symmetries are C92's.
  - The same two bounds hold for every x_j with j < n of the same block
    (proved, not checked).
  - Consequence: W/2^q ≤ 1/4 + 2^(1−q).
- **Lower bound, closed form (a = 2; proved for all n, §5 Theorem 5.3).**
  - A parity E(n, N) decides whether the degree-(2n + 1) monomial
    e·b_0⋯b_{n−1}·x_0⋯x_{n−1} is in the ANF of f.
  - Corollary 5.4: for every even n ≥ 2 and N = 2^n − 1 it is present, so
    deg f ≥ 2n + 1 and, by the TX52 lemma, W(f) ≥ 2^(2n+1).
  - For odd n and N = 2^n − 1 that monomial is absent.
- **Lower bound, verified at listed sizes (a = 2, Corollary 5.5).**
  - deg f ≥ 2n + 1 for every odd N at n = 2..10 except nine: N = 31, 41,
    295, 303, 311, 319, 327, 335 and 511.
  - At the first eight the bound is 2n; at N = 511 it is 2n − 1.
  - This is exact evaluation of the proved slice formula (Lemma C), checked
    against the circuit at n = 2, 3.
- **Top-degree criterion (proved, §9.1 Lemma T; checked in review
  V16acbf38c34b4414).** deg f = 3n + 2 if and only if one of n + 1 explicit
  top monomials T_j is present.
- **Reach of the degree route (proved, §3 Corollary 3.4).** Any degree
  argument certifies at most W ≥ 2^(3n+2), which is half the proved ceiling.
- **Growth the lower bounds certify: 4^n.**
  - Proved for all n along N = 2^n − 1 with n even (Corollary 5.4).
  - Verified at listed sizes for the other odd N with n ≤ 10, apart from the
    nine exceptions (Corollary 5.5).
- **Comparison with C102 (measured; a consistency reading of older data, not
  a test).**
  - C102's 42 archived W values are all even and lie in
    [2^(3n+2), 2^(3n+3) + 2].
  - Its median W grows about like 8^n, and W/2^q rises towards the proved
    ceiling of 1/4.
  - The degree cap 2^(3n+2) is 0.664–0.719 of W at n = 3 and 0.50–0.54 at
    n = 4..8, recomputed from `out/heisenberg_bdd/report_v1.json` and
    `n8_report_v1.json` (42 fixtures).

## Attainment of the degree cap (verified at listed sizes; a = 2)

Experiment task T066df2c305284ceb, submission S1f5d074246394b33, accepted in
V69a199ae5c5a4984. The referee reproduced every number by an independent
route. Frozen tables: `out/agent-board/workers/Adbf05967c8a5413a/tables_v1.txt`.

- **n = 4..7, exact ANF.** deg f = 3n + 2 at all 36 fixtures (C102's moduli:
  4 at n = 4, 8 at n = 5, 16 at n = 6, 8 at n = 7).
- **n = 3.** deg f is 9 (N = 5) and 10 (N = 7), against a cap of 11.
- **n = 8..10, via Lemma T.** Exact parities show that some top monomial T_j
  is present, so deg f = 3n + 2 by Lemma T and the ceiling. The moduli are:
  - n = 8: N = 129, 163, 197, 251;
  - n = 9: N = 257, 259, 385, 511;
  - n = 10: N = 513, 515, 769, 1023;
  - generic moduli, reported separately: N = 403, 437 (n = 9) and 811, 877
    (n = 10).
  - Every one of these 16 has two independent routes: the interval route, and
    the referee's exhaustive replay of Lemma T's fibre formula.
- **Consequence.** At each of the 52 moduli with n ≥ 4 above, the TX52 lemma
  gives W ≥ 2^(3n+2), which is exactly the degree route's cap. That is half the
  proved ceiling, and includes n = 9, 10, where W has never been computed.
- **Not every top monomial sits at one index.** From n = 5 on, some T_j with
  j < n is present at many fixtures, and at some fixtures T_n itself is
  absent. The cap is reached through different T_j at different N.
- **Three of Corollary 5.5's exceptions are settled.** At N = 31 (n = 5),
  41 (n = 6) and 511 (n = 9), deg f = 3n + 2, above the weaker slice bound
  that Corollary 5.5 gives there.

## Not reached

- The exact degree for general n, or its top coefficient (§10 gap 1). The cap
  3n + 2 is attained at every modulus tested with 4 ≤ n ≤ 10 (36 by exact
  ANF, 16 via Lemma T; above), and not at n = 3 or at the n = 2 instance
  (§8). No general-n argument exists, and most moduli at n ≥ 7 were not
  tested.
- A lower bound valid for every N at every n (§10 gap 2).
- Why ±g1's spectrum nearly fills its allowed coset, which is the measured
  rise of W/2^q towards 1/4 (§10 gap 3).
- Multi-block builds and other observables beyond x_j, j < n (§10 gap 4).

Degree gives no upper bound on sparsity (TX52); the ceiling here comes from
the symmetries.
